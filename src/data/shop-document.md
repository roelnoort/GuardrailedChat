## Shipping

- Free shipping in the Netherlands and Belgium on orders above €150; otherwise €9.95.
- Rest of EU: €19.95 per parcel.
- Heavy items (racks, rigs, cardio machines, plate sets over 100 kg) ship as freight: €49 in NL/BE, €99 elsewhere in the EU. Freight is delivered curbside within 5–10 working days.
- Regular orders ship within 1–2 working days.

## Returns & Warranty

- 30-day return policy for unused items in original packaging. Customer pays return shipping, except for defective items.
- Freight items can be returned, but a €99 pickup fee applies.
- Warranty: 2 years on all products, 10 years on rack and rig frames, lifetime on barbell shafts against bending under normal use.
